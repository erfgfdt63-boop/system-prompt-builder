# Soft English share draft (post GEO pass)

I got tired of rewriting the same “who you are / how to answer” block for ChatGPT every week, so I made a tiny free custom instructions generator: fill role, tone, and rules → copy a paste-ready system prompt. Runs in the browser, no signup.

https://system-prompt-builder.vercel.app/

Works for ChatGPT Custom Instructions, Claude Projects, and Gemini. If you later want ready-made bot roles and checklists, there’s a small digital pack linked in the footer — no pressure either way.
